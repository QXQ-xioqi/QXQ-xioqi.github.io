# Xiaoqi Qin

> One world. Many views. Long rollouts.

Hi! I am Xiaoqi, a Mechatronics Engineering undergraduate at the [South China University of Technology](https://www.scut.edu.cn/en/) and a research intern in [Prof. Hao Tang's group](https://ha0tang.github.io/) at Peking University.

I work on **video world models**, **World-Action Models**, and **embodied intelligence**. The question keeping me busy right now is simple to ask and hard to solve:

**Can a generative model keep one coherent world alive while multiple observers move, act, and look from different places?**

My current project adapts _LingBot-World_, a 14B action-conditioned video DiT, for multi-view interactive generation in highly dynamic CS:GO scenes. It is an ongoing industry collaboration with [YAHAHA](https://yahaha.com/).
