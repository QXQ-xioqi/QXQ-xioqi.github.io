I am **Xiaoqi Qin (秦筱淇)**, a third-year undergraduate in Mechatronics Engineering at [South China University of Technology](https://www.scut.edu.cn/en/) (SCUT). I am currently a research intern in [Prof. Hao Tang's group](https://ha0tang.github.io/) at Peking University, where I work on generative world models and embodied intelligence.

My research interests center on **video world models**, **World-Action Models (WAM)**, and **embodied AI**: building generative models that simulate coherent, controllable worlds, coupling prediction with action, and ultimately grounding them in agents that reason and act in physical environments.

## Current research

I study **map-conditioned multi-view first-person interactive world modeling**. Our project adapts _LingBot-World_, a 14B action-conditioned video Diffusion Transformer, to highly dynamic CS:GO scenes with rapid camera motion and abrupt visual changes. The resulting system generates synchronized viewpoints with cross-view consistency and a stable world state, while supporting complex action-conditioned interactions and long-horizon generation.

Within this collaboration, I designed a residual **Map Memory** conditioning adapter with LoRA, built the end-to-end data and evaluation pipeline, and trained and debugged the 14B model on a 16× NVIDIA H200 GPU cluster. This work is ongoing; a manuscript and a fuller project page are in preparation. See the current [project overview](/projects/multi-view-interactive-world-model/) and my [CV](/cv/) for details.
