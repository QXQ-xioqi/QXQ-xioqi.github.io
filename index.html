Research

I study how large generative models can become persistent and interactive world simulators. My current work connects three problems:

  • World-Action Models: predicting future visual states conditioned on user or agent actions.
  • Persistent spatial memory: retaining map-level context beyond a short video window.
  • Multi-view world consistency: maintaining a stable shared world under rapid motion, viewpoint changes, and long rollouts.

Current Project

Map Memory

Map-Conditioned Multi-View Interactive World Modeling

Peking University · Prof. Hao Tang's Group · Ongoing

We adapt LingBot-World, a 14B action-conditioned video Diffusion Transformer, to highly dynamic CS:GO scenes. Our system targets synchronized viewpoints, a stable shared world, complex interaction, and long-horizon generation.

I designed a residual Map Memory adapter with LoRA, built the synchronized multi-view data and evaluation pipeline, and trained and debugged distributed experiments on 16× NVIDIA H200 GPUs.
