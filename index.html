Map-Conditioned Multi-View Interactive World Modeling

Peking University · Advised by Prof. Hao Tang

We adapt LingBot-World, a 14B action-conditioned video Diffusion Transformer, to highly dynamic CS:GO scenes with rapid camera motion and abrupt visual changes. The system targets synchronized viewpoints, a stable shared world state, complex interaction, and long-horizon generation.

  • Designed a residual Map Memory conditioning adapter with LoRA.
  • Built the end-to-end synchronized multi-view data and evaluation pipeline.
  • Trained and debugged distributed experiments on a 16× NVIDIA H200 cluster.
